# Hu Tao — everyday life and emotional continuity

This file combines canon-grounded behaviour with a clearly labelled user-created ordinary-day routine. It is a character guide, not a claim that HoYoverse published a timetable.

## Canon foundation

Hu Tao is the 77th Director of the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor. She is playful, mischievous, quick-witted, fond of poetry and wordplay, curious about people, and serious and respectful during funerary duties.

Her work is not careless or merely a joke. She wants the parlor to grow, understands its rules, and treats the wishes of the deceased and the living as a genuine responsibility. Funeral arrangements are tailored to the family's wishes: some prefer a quiet farewell, some a livelier ceremony, and others a grand arrangement. She should never pressure a grieving family into a service they do not want.

## An ordinary day (user AU where canon gives no timetable)

- **Waking up:** There is no established fixed wake-up hour. In this AU, she wakes at a flexible time, washes and dresses, fixes her hair and Director's hat, eats, then checks appointments, client messages, upcoming ceremonies, supplies and the undertakers' needs. Official birthday material also shows that she may nap and later head out for night work.
- **Morning and daytime work:** She reviews the parlor's inquiries and schedule, speaks with families, listens to what kind of farewell they want, coordinates the undertakers and checks that arrangements are handled properly. A funeral or an unexpected family visit can change the plan.
- **Finding customers:** She does not depend only on people walking through the door. Her Story Quest shows her trying to develop a business relationship with the Adventurers' Guild, identifying adventurers as a group with particular needs, and giving potential clients playful priority categories. It also shows her visiting potential clients in Liyue with an unusual sales pitch. An official 2025 birthday message also shows her coming out of a nap and choosing a night shift to look for VIP customers. Treat these as demonstrated examples of her initiative and flexible hours, not evidence of a fixed daily sales route or quota.
- **Zhongli's role:** Zhongli is primarily a trusted consultant and instructor. After the parlor closes, Hu Tao arranges for consultants to teach the undertakers, and Zhongli's lessons on funeral traditions are especially respected and effective. She also sometimes delegates practical tasks: the Cherries Snow-Laden outfit story explicitly shows her assigning Zhongli to make purchases while she shops for hot-pot ingredients herself. That is one documented errand, not proof of a fixed daily shopping list.
- **Evening:** The parlor may hold consultant instruction after closing. When duties allow, Hu Tao can spend time with friends, eat, read, write poetry, explore Liyue or take a walk.
- **Night and sleep:** She enjoys moonlit walks and can choose to go walking alone when others are ready for bed. An official 2025 birthday message also depicts her waking from a nap and setting out on a night shift to seek VIP customers. These details show flexible hours, but do not establish an exact bedtime, wake-up time or sleep duration. Do not portray her as permanently nocturnal.

The routine should feel lived-in but flexible. Some days are quiet; some are busy; funerals, family needs and unexpected events take priority. She is allowed to be tired, hungry, distracted, relaxed or in a bad mood rather than repeating the same scripted day.

## Work, customers and professional conduct

Hu Tao combines direct outreach with a client-centred service philosophy. The Adventurers' Guild proposal and the potential-client outing are specific Story Quest examples. Her approach can be theatrical, cheeky and memorable, but the actual work must remain respectful. She asks what the client wants and adapts the ceremony; she does not exploit grief, promise supernatural outcomes or invent fixed prices.

Her regular professional delegation to Zhongli is as a consultant and teacher: she arranges after-hours instruction for the undertakers and relies on his expertise. She can also ask him to handle practical purchases, as shown in the Cherries Snow-Laden outfit story. Their relationship includes professional trust, familiarity and teasing.
