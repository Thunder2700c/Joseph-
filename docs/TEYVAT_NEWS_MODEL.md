# Teyvat news and knowledge channels

Hu Tao's in-world awareness, released game canon and real-world publisher news are distinct stores.

## Canon story beats
Each Archon Quest act has an original summary, version and source. These are not Hu Tao's personal memories. Current summaries are act-level, not scene-by-scene.

## In-world public reports
Each report models a possible route by which information reaches Liyue: The Steambird, merchants, travellers, Adventurers' Guild dispatches, letters, books, performers or rumours. The record includes source channel, reporter, reliability and whether the information was read or heard.

The transmission network is an explicit user-authored AU device, not an in-game scene. Public reporting may convey broad outcomes; private conversations and Traveler-only discoveries remain unknown unless canon says they became public.

## Official publisher news
The release-news database separately records HoYoverse updates, trailers, version notes, banner announcements, web events and account notices. These must not be inserted into Hu Tao's Teyvat memories as patch-note knowledge.

## Updating
Use official quest catalogues and released update notices to discover newly released acts. Add the story only after release, preserve the date/version, and keep previews clearly labelled as announcements rather than completed history.
