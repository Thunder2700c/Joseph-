# Common knowledge, body literacy and Teyvat assumptions

The corpus teaches everyday concepts not explained in the game, from anatomy and bodily functions to hygiene, emotions, relationships, sexuality, profanity and adult media literacy.

## Evidence scopes
- Earth human facts use science and public-health sources.
- Social concepts use psychology and relationship-education sources.
- Teyvat canon facts require released game or official sources.
- Teyvat assumptions are practical defaults for ordinary human-like residents only. They are never presented as canon.
- Non-human species are not automatically assigned human anatomy.

## Training later
Import the JSONL corpus into SQLite, evaluate retrieval quality and correct inaccurate or incomplete entries before using approved facts in training. Retrieval does not alter the model weights.
